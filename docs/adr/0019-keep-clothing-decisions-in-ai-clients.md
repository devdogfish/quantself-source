# Keep Clothing Decisions in AI Clients

Quantself owns clothing facts, wear state, and physical placement, while authorized AI Clients make daily and event-specific outfit decisions through the Quantself API. The dedicated Clothing page presents the recommendation, alternatives, reasoning, and planned event outfits; it does not contain a deterministic recommendation engine. A saved outfit does not change clothing wear state; one explicit owner confirmation that it was worn marks its clothing items dirty, with undo, and laundry completion bulk-marks clothing items clean.
