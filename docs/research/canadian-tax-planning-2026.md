# 2026 Canadian tax planning in Nova Scotia

Reviewed November 8, 2026. For personal returns covering January 1 through December 31, 2026. The provincial guidance applies to filers residing in Nova Scotia on December 31. Some CRA guides linked below still display 2025 forms. Use the 2026 package when it is published. Self-employed filers generally have until June 15, 2027 to file, with payment due April 30, 2027. [CRA 2026 filing deadlines](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/taxes/resources-for-small-and-medium-businesses/2026-tax-deadlines-canadian-businesses-self-employed-individuals.html) [CRA on tax years](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/educational-programs/starting-work.html) · [CRA on provincial residence](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/personal-address-information/your-province-territory-residence.html)

## Employment and refund estimate

- Collect year-to-date taxable gross and income tax withheld from the last 2026 stub of each employer, plus CPP, CPP2, EI, employee pension contributions, union dues and eligible employee-paid health plan premiums. Project any remaining taxable pay. Once available, use T4 boxes 14, 22, 16/16A, 18, 20, 44 and code 85 where applicable. Payroll income tax withheld is a payment toward the year's liability. Apply 2026 rates to actual taxable income after deductions, then apply credits and reconcile with withholding. [CRA T4 guide](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/tax-slips/understand-your-tax-slips/t4-slips/t4-statement-remuneration-paid.html) · [CRA 2026 brackets](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/tax-rates-brackets/current-year.html)
- Required, unreimbursed employment expenses may qualify with employer-certified T2200 and a T777 calculation. Ordinary commuting, groceries, lunches and clothing are personal. Travel in the course of duties can qualify. Ordinary salaried travel meals generally require 12 consecutive hours away from the usual workplace municipality and metropolitan area, where applicable, and are generally subject to a 50% limit. Record dates, routes, purpose, receipts and reimbursements. [CRA T4044](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/t4044/employment-expenses.html)
- Employee home office eligibility generally requires an arrangement to work from home and either more than 50% of work time at home for at least four consecutive weeks or exclusive use with regular, ongoing in-person work meetings. Allocate eligible rent, utilities and internet using workspace area, shared use and eligible periods. Avoid claiming the same costs as business expenses. [CRA detailed method](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/line-22900-other-employment-expenses/work-space-home-expenses/who-claim/detailed-method.html)
- If a move for work made the new home at least 40 km closer to the new work location by the shortest public route, check moving costs against income earned at that location. Collect old and new distances, eligible travel, mover/storage and lease cancellation receipts, and reimbursements. [CRA moving expenses](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/line-21900-moving-expenses.html)

## Freelance and startup costs

- Keep contract, description of services, client country, invoice and earned/payment dates, gross foreign-currency amount, fees, reimbursements, any foreign tax withheld and payment evidence. Report Canadian-dollar gross revenue and allowable expenses on T2125. Generally use accrual accounting for freelance services and the Bank of Canada rate when each amount arises, with possible separate exchange gain or loss on later settlement. [CRA T4002](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/t4002/t4002-3.html) · [CRA T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html) · [CRA currency folio](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/technical-information/income-tax/income-tax-folios-index/series-5-international-residency/series-5-international-residency-folio-4-foreign-currency/income-tax-folio-s5-f4-c1-income-tax-reporting-currency.html)
- Screen reasonable business shares of software, hosting, payment fees, domain costs, supplies, phone/internet, and trips to clients or suppliers. Log ride receipts and business purpose, or business and total kilometres for a personal vehicle. Eligible client meals are usually limited to 50%. Ordinary living costs are personal. For startup costs, establish when each business commenced through concrete activity, which taxpayer incurred the costs and how each cost supports the relevant business. [CRA T2125 expenses](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/completing-form-t2125/expenses-section-form-t2125.html) · [CRA business start-up costs](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/business-expenses/business-start-costs.html)
- Business workspace costs require a principal place of business or exclusive use for income and regular, continuous client/customer/patient meetings. Prorate rent and eligible costs by area and time where applicable. This claim cannot create or increase a loss. Unused amounts can carry forward if conditions continue to be met. [CRA business-use-of-home expenses](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/completing-form-t2125/business-use-home-expenses.html)
- Purchased server hardware is generally capital property. General-purpose servers may be Class 50 (55%) and qualifying data-network infrastructure may be Class 46 (30%). Bill C-15 received royal assent. Section 103 allows up to 100% first-year CCA for qualifying property acquired after April 15, 2024 and available for use before 2027. Check hardware function, ownership, prior use/transfer restrictions, date first available for income-producing use, business-use share and short fiscal period proration. Hosting service fees generally follow the current expense rules. [Enacted Bill C-15](https://www.parl.ca/DocumentViewer/en/13996732) · [CRA CCA classes](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/t4012/t2-corporation-income-tax-guide-chapter-3-page-3-t2-return.html) · [CRA CCA calculation](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/t4002/t4002-6.html#toc3)
- Check aggregate worldwide taxable supplies, including zero-rated supplies, against the $30,000 small-supplier test. GST/HST treatment for a non-resident client depends on the service and recipient. [CRA GST/HST registration](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html)

## Incorporation

Incorporation changes which taxpayer earns income and claims costs. A subsequent corporation does not automatically acquire earlier freelance income, expenses or personal losses. Model the sole-proprietor scenario against an incorporated scenario using forecast profit, amount retained versus withdrawn, salary/dividend treatment and compliance costs. Qualifying active business income can face 9% federal plus 1.5% Nova Scotia corporate tax. Include personal tax on withdrawals when comparing. A resident corporation generally files a T2 annually even if inactive. Nova Scotia lists $200 to incorporate and $118.35 to renew. For existing equipment, document title, original cost, CCA claimed and fair market value before transferring. A joint subsection 85(1) election on T2057 may defer tax on an eligible transfer. [CRA corporate rates](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/corporations/corporation-tax-rates.html) · [CRA T2 filing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/corporations/corporation-income-tax-return/who-file-a-corporation-income-tax-return.html) · [Nova Scotia incorporation fees](https://www.novascotia.ca/incorporate-limited-company) · [CRA section 85 transfers](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/ic76-19/transfer-property-a-corporation-under-section-85.html)

## Personal credits and benefits

- Dental care and medically indicated orthodontics can count toward the non-refundable medical expense tax credit. For a medical expense claim, keep itemized receipts, payment dates and insurance statements where applicable. Compare eligible unreimbursed expenses across any unused 12-month window ending in 2026. The federal floor is the lesser of 3% of net income or $2,890. Check the provincial calculation and refundable medical expense supplement separately. Medical transport can qualify at 40 km one way if substantially equivalent care was unavailable closer and other conditions are met. At 80 km one way, eligible meals, parking and lodging can also qualify. [CRA RC4065](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/rc4065/medical-expenses.html) · [2026 federal threshold](https://www.budget.canada.ca/2026/report-rapport/tm-mf-en.html) · [CRA medical supplement](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/line-45200-refundable-medical-expense-supplement.html)
- For an eligible tuition claim, use the T2202 and verify employer reimbursements and prior carryforwards. Check federal Schedule 11 and Nova Scotia Schedule NS(S11). Interest on qualifying government student loans may be eligible for a separate credit. Check the refundable Canada Training Credit if the assessment shows a limit and eligibility conditions are met. [CRA P105](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/p105/p105-students-income-tax.html) · [Nova Scotia return guide](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/tax-packages-years/general-income-tax-benefit-package/nova-scotia/5003-pc.html) · [CRA training credit](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/line-45350-canada-training-credit.html)
- Rent and other ordinary living costs are generally personal. Separately screen for the income-tested Nova Scotia rent supplement, Affordable Living Tax Credit, Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit and Heating Assistance Rebate. Canada Workers Benefit generally excludes full-time students enrolled for more than 13 weeks unless they have an eligible dependant. If the work and age qualify, check Nova Scotia's MOST program for designated skilled trades, nursing and film occupations. ## Reading the dashboard

- **Refund collected** is an amount actually received for the selected tax year. Record it with the assessment or deposit evidence even if the money arrived the following year. Payroll deposits and periodic benefit payments belong in their own entries.
- **Refund allocation** is a reconciliation you enter by spending category. Keep the remainder unallocated if you lack a basis for the split. A tax assessment reports the overall outcome, which can include excess withholding as well as deductions and credits.
- **Estimated savings** are entered from a tax calculation. To measure one claim, compare the same return with and without it. If claims interact through brackets, thresholds, or credit limits, document the order used and avoid adding overlapping estimates.
- **Recorded spending** uses the recorded CAD costs. Review business purpose, reimbursements, timing, and eligible use before using a cost in the tax calculation.

## Additional checks

- A genuine sole-proprietor business loss can generally reduce other income. A corporate loss generally stays with the corporation. Consider whether claiming the maximum CCA this year gives a better result than keeping part of the balance for later. [Income Tax Act, section 3](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/I-3.3/section-3.html) · [CRA CCA guide](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/t4002/t4002-6.html)
- For foreign income, collect proof of any foreign tax withheld and confirm the source of the income and applicable treaty before claiming a foreign tax credit. Eligible GST/HST registrants can check input tax credits separately from income-tax deductions. [CRA foreign tax credit](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2209.html) · [CRA input tax credits](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/calculate-prepare-report/input-tax-credit.html)
- Filers without access to private dental coverage can check Canadian Dental Care Plan eligibility. Review the current list of covered services before booking. [Eligibility](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/dental/dental-care-plan/qualify.html) · [Coverage](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/dental/dental-care-plan/coverage.html)
