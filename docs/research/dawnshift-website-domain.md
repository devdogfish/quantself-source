# Dawnshift website domain: dawnshiftagency.com vs dawnshift.agency vs dawnshift.ai

Verified 2026-11-14. Dawnshift sells outcomes, not processes, so the website domain should not lead with "AI". All three candidates are owned (registered 2026-11-14).

## Recommendation

Use **dawnshiftagency.com** for the website, brand and client email. Redirect dawnshift.agency and dawnshift.ai to it and send no mail from them.

- **People assume .com, and dawnshift.com belongs to someone else.** Anyone who hears "Dawnshift dot agency" and types .com lands on that site, and mail sent there by habit goes to its owner.
- **Putting "AI" in the address lowers trust**, especially for expensive purchases.
- **The .com has the lowest price and registry risk.**
- **Deliverability doesn't decide it.** All three are clean.

## Name conflict

- dawnshift.com was registered in 2004. It's locked against transfer and expires 2027-10-06. ([RDAP](https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/dawnshift.com))
- It hosts a live WordPress gaming blog with no for-sale notice. The last post was in Sept 2022. ([site](http://www.dawnshift.com), [posts API](https://www.dawnshift.com/wp-json/wp/v2/posts?per_page=3))
- A web search found no US "DAWNSHIFT" trademark. The nearest marks are DOWNSHIFT and DAYSHIFT ([Justia](https://trademarks.justia.com/994/27/downshift-99427344.html)).
- **Canada is not checked.** Search [CIPO](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/cipo/trademark-search/srch?lang=eng&text=dawnshift) and [USPTO](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov) by hand.

## Buyer perception and memorability

- **ICANN/Nielsen, 2016, n=5,452:**
  - 95% knew .com.
  - 38% in North America knew any new gTLD.
  - 91% trusted legacy TLDs.
  - People were less comfortable giving personal information to new-gTLD sites.
  - ([ICANN](https://www.icann.org/en/announcements/details/icann-commissioned-study-finds-increased-awareness-and-trust-in-domain-name-system-23-6-2016-en))
- **Interbrand, about 2015, n≈1,000, paid for by Verisign:**
  - 81% assumed a new-TLD site was a .com.
  - 94% recalled a .com address correctly, against 7% for a new extension.
  - ([MarTech summary](https://martech.org/verisign-com-domains-have-more-click-appeal-in-search-results); the original PDF wasn't found)
- **Registrations:**
  - .com: about 166.6M ([Verisign DNIB Q2 2026](https://blog.verisign.com/domain-names/q2-2026-domain-name-industry-brief-quarterly-report/)).
  - .ai: over 1M ([Hogan Lovells](https://www.hoganlovells.com/en/publications/ai-reaches-the-1-million-mark)).
  - .agency: about 123K and shrinking (third-party zone data, [Instant Domain Search](https://instantdomainsearch.com/trends)).
- **Seer Interactive, 2014, n=200:** short domains were recalled about 9.9x more often, and .com gave no recall advantage once length was controlled for. ([Seer](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/when-it-comes-to-urls-does-size-matter))
  - In favour of dawnshiftagency.com anyway: it is two real words that are easy to spell aloud (inference).

## "AI" in the brand

- **WSU, 2024, n>1,000 US consumers:** calling a product "AI" lowered emotional trust and purchase intention. The effect was stronger for high-risk purchases. ([WSU](https://news.wsu.edu/press-release/2024/07/30/using-the-term-artificial-intelligence-in-product-descriptions-reduces-purchase-intentions/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/19368623.2024.2368040)) The sample was consumers, not B2B buyers.
- **Pew, 2025, n=5,023:** 50% of US adults are more concerned than excited about AI, against 10% more excited. ([Pew](https://www.pewresearch.org/science/2025/09/17/how-americans-view-ai-and-its-impact-on-people-and-society/))
- **Gartner, 2024:** 64% of customers would prefer companies not use AI in customer service. This came from the headline only; the page couldn't be read. ([Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2024-07-09-gartner-survey-finds-64-percent-of-customers-would-prefer-that-companies-didnt-use-ai-for-customer-service))

## Deliverability

- **Gmail:** its sender requirements don't mention TLDs. ([Google](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/81126))
- **Microsoft 365:** no global TLD penalty, but an organization's admins can block an entire TLD (`*.TLD`). ([Microsoft](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/defender-office-365/tenant-allow-block-list-email-spoof-configure)) New gTLDs are likelier targets (inference; no data found).
- **Spamhaus, 4-week data pulled 2026-11-14:**
  - .com: about 0.41% of observed domains were spam.
  - .agency: about 0.53%.
  - .ai: about 0.15%.
  - None of the three is a high-abuse TLD. ([gTLDs](https://www.spamhaus.org/reputation-statistics/gtlds/spam/), [ccTLDs](https://www.spamhaus.org/reputation-statistics/cctlds/spam/))
- **SES:** no TLD-specific requirements. ([AWS](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/ses/latest/dg/troubleshoot-verification.html))

## Registry and price risk

- **.com:** run by Verisign. Wholesale is $10.97 from 2026-11-01, and increases are capped by contract. ([Verisign](https://investor.verisign.com/news-releases/news-release-details/verisign-reports-first-quarter-2026-results))
- **.agency:** run by Identity Digital. ICANN's agreement requires 180 days' notice of price increases and the same renewal price for everyone. It was raised again 2026-10-06, to $28.04 per Dynadot. ([ICANN RA §2.10](https://itp.cdn.icann.org/en/files/registry-agreements/base-registry-agreement-12-03-2026-en.pdf), [Dynadot](https://www.dynadot.com/blog/price-changes-for-over-200-identity-digital-tlds))
- **.ai:**
  - It's a ccTLD managed by the Government of Anguilla, with no ICANN price cap and no notice requirement. ([IANA](https://www.iana.org/domains/root/db/ai.html))
  - Anguilla's terms let it deny, suspend, cancel, redirect or transfer any registration "at any time and without limitation". ([nic.ai FAQ](https://www.nic.ai/faq))
  - Registration is 2 years minimum. Wholesale is about $160 per 2 years after a rise in 2026; this figure is from a secondary source. ([Domain Name Wire](https://domainnamewire.com/2026/02/02/ai-domain-name-prices-going-up-20/))
